Support

Questions, bug reports, and feature requests all go to the same place. Since the app has no account and no server, we can only see what you tell us.

Contact

Email dottedcrane@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. Include your device model and Android version if you're reporting a bug — see below for what else helps.

Before you write in

  • Most "how do I…" questions are answered in the usage guide — it covers the maintenance hub, vehicles and the garage, building and furnishing the 3D home, and camera controls.
  • Wondering what the app stores or sends anywhere? Short answer: nothing leaves your device unless you export it. Full detail in the privacy policy.
  • Lost data after an update or a restore? Check whether you have a Backup App Data export saved — Restore App Data overwrites everything currently on the device, so a recent backup is the fastest way back.

Filing a good bug report

Since there's no crash reporting or analytics of any kind (see the privacy policy), we only know what you send us. The more of this you include, the faster it gets fixed:

  • Device model and Android version.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • Steps to reproduce it — the exact taps, in order, if you can reconstruct them.
  • Whether it happens every time or only sometimes, and whether it started after a specific action (an update, a restore, adding a specific item or vehicle).
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if the bug is visual.

Feature requests

Send them the same way. Tell us what you're trying to do and where the app got in your way — the specific task or item you needed and couldn't track is more useful than a general description of the feature.
