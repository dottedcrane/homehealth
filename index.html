The usage model

What you build determines what you maintain

You don't fill out forms about your house — you build it. The maintenance hub watches what exists and schedules only that. Add a pool, and pool tasks appear. Remove the deck, and deck tasks disappear. Park a car, and oil changes join the list.

1

Claim your home

Start in a 3D neighborhood: preview five model homes or build your own lot. Claim one, enter the year built and year purchased, and it saves on your device.

2

Make it yours

Edit the floor plan room by room, toggle features (garage, pool, deck, yard) and systems (HVAC, solar), drag furniture into rooms, and park your cars, boat, or motorcycle on the lot.

3

The checklist writes itself

Every task is gated by what your home actually has. Each physical thing — "Water Heater", "Car 1", "Car 2" — is tracked as its own instance with its own history.

4

Maintain by season, forever

Mark a task done and the next occurrence lands in the next real season — it never ends, because home maintenance never does. One tap hands any task to your own calendar app.

Two independent signals

Age is not the same as upkeep

Every tracked item shows two badges that answer two different questions. A water heater can be well within its lifespan while its annual flush is a year overdue — Casa Health shows you both, side by side.

ABC

Lifespan rating

How far into (or past) its expected service life the item is, computed from its install year — or your home's build year until you set one.

Upkeep status

Whether the item's recurring maintenance is current, overdue, or has never been done — driven by your actual "Mark Done" history, not the calendar's optimism.

The maintenance hub

Four tabs, one wrench icon

✅

Checklist

Every applicable task sorted by due date and anchored to real calendar seasons — monthly rolls, quarterly hits Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec, annual lands in the season that fits the job. Mark done, or hand it to your calendar app.

📋

Inventory

Everything tracked, grouped by what matters: items needing maintenance on top, then vehicles, then structural assets (roof, siding, plumbing, panel…). Each row carries both badges and its own documents.

🚗

Vehicles & garage

Drag cars, boats, and motorcycles onto your lot. Tap the garage door to step into its own 3D scene — the door rolls open and your parked car pulls out. Every vehicle gets its own maintenance card and tasks.

🛠️

Guides

Step-by-step DIY repair guides — warning signs, numbered steps, safety warnings, prevention tips, and an honest "call a professional" line for every one.

📇

Pros

Your own list of trusted service contacts — plumber, electrician, HVAC tech — typed in by you and stored only on your device. The app never reads your contact book.

💾

Backup & restore

Export your entire home — database, records, documents — as a single .zip file wherever you choose. Restore it on any device. That export is the only way data ever leaves the app.

Requirements

Runs on the phone you already have

No cloud dependency and no minimum-spec surprise — Casa Health is built to run entirely on-device.

🤖

Android 7.0+

API level 24 (Nougat) or later.

📶

No internet required

Offline-first. Works fully in airplane mode — the app makes no network requests of its own.

Privacy

Nothing to collect. Nowhere to send it.

Casa Health has no server, so your data has no destination. Your floor plan, install years, task history, contacts, and documents live in the app's private storage on your phone — and work exactly the same in airplane mode.

A note on safety

Reminders are ours. Safe conditions are yours.

Many of the tasks Casa Health reminds you about — garage doors, pool chemicals, vehicle fluids, electrical panels — involve real hazards, especially to children. The usage guide includes plain-language child-safety warnings for every one of them. Please read them before your first weekend of checklist-clearing.
